# Website package budgets

Website packages are annual third-party subscription estimates. They do not price Landseaire logistics services or website development work.

| Package | Hosting | Hosting estimate | .com domain | Total before email |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Option A — Basic | Hostinger Single | ₱3,348 | ₱1,019 | ₱4,367 |
| Option B — Business | Hostinger Premium | ₱5,628 | ₱1,019 | ₱6,647 |
| Option C — Advanced | Hostinger Unlimited | ₱6,228 | ₱1,019 | ₱7,247 |

Google Workspace is independently selectable for all website packages. The calculator supports Business Starter, Standard, and Plus, 1–20 licensed users, and annual-commitment or flexible billing. Selecting Business Plus does not require selecting Advanced hosting.

Each licensed Workspace user is a separate business Gmail mailbox. Aliases that deliver into an existing mailbox generally do not need another license. SSL, Resend Free, and Cloudflare Free are included as ₱0 assumptions. A domain is always a separately paid annual line item.

Figures are planning estimates, not offers, contracts, renewal quotations, or verified checkout totals.
